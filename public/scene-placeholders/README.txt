Layout for replacement artwork:
- Side-on camera, 1280 x 720 canvas.
- Keep the walkable ground surface horizontal at y = 422 pixels from the top.
- Refugees escape towards the left; zombies approach from the right.
- Elowen is displayed at 96 pixels high while standing.
- Keep space above the ground clear enough for sword combat and jumping.

These PNGs are flat reference exports. Town, grasslands and road backgrounds are currently drawn in code. The bridge uses forest.png with scenery overlays. These files do not replace the current game backgrounds automatically.
