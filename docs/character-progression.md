# Elowen’s character progression

Source: user-provided **Chaosbound – Core Rules**, Champion’s “Stamina Points per Level: 12”, the Character Progression table, Proficiency, Health, and the Weapons table.

Elowen fights wave one at level one and gains one level before each subsequent wave. Automatic level benefits are additional to the player's one permanent spell or Champion upgrade choice. There are ten levels and nine upgrade choices. Victory does not grant level eleven.

## Source rules and real-time adaptations

- SP is health, as defined in the source. The regenerating stamina resource remains the separate cost of blocking and Champion actions.
- Preserve the prototype's 100 starting health and 27 starting sword damage. Each new level adds 12 maximum and current SP; the existing 22 SP recovery between waves follows, capped at the new maximum. Injuries are not automatically fully healed.
- Proficiency tiers and bonuses follow levels 1, 2, 5, 8, and 10. Proficiency normally affects checks, saves, and attack rolls. The game records the tier/bonus; it has no tabletop accuracy rolls, so this bonus does not add further damage or random misses.
- Extra weapon damage dice are cumulative: one at level 2, two at level 8, three at level 10. Each longsword die becomes its fixed one-handed d10 average (5.5 damage). Sentinel's returning shield uses the printed d4 average (2.5 damage per bonus die). These bonuses combine with existing sword buffs and shield ability ranks. Allied Rallying Cry arrows and spells do not receive Elowen's weapon bonuses.
- Follow the main Character Progression table's **total** Basic AR Bonus: +1 from level 2, +2 from level 8, retained at level 10. In tabletop combat AR affects hit rolls; here each bonus point reduces enemy attack damage by one, with a zero floor, before Warrior's Shield halves it. Battle Focus still prevents all damage. Bridge breach damage remains 20.
- Level statistics are calculated from the attained level, so repeated setup does not stack milestone bonuses. Restart restores all level-one statistics.
